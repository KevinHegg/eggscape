Eleven rooms of the kitchen

The Great Eggscape

The Queen wants an omelette. The Egg King wants his mother.

He falls through her kitchen by himself. All you can do is throw straw: tap, and a bale lands there a moment later. Straw is soft. Nothing else in a kitchen is. And keep him moving: if he stops, the Queen's hook comes down for him.

A hen keeps a roost at the bottom of each room. Once he is on it he is safe, and a fall further down only sends him back that far.
